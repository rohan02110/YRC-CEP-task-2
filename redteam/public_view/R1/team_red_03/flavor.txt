KURUKSHETRA CTF — CHALLENGE 3 (HARD)
THE CHAKRAVYUHA CIPHER: SEALED MACHINE
================================================================================

On the thirteenth day of the great Kurukshetra War, Acharya Drona arrayed the
Kaurava host in the impenetrable Chakravyuha: a multi-tiered, ever-turning spiral
labyrinth designed to trap and crush all who enter without mastering its laws.

Eighteen days of destiny divide the ages; eighteen celestial akshauhinis clashed
upon the sacred field. The epic song records eighteen cantos of divine dialogue
between Arjuna, paralyzed by doubt, and Krishna, the divine charioteer whose words
restored the warrior's soul. Sanjaya, granted divine sight by the sage Vyasa,
narrated the clash from afar, while righteous Yudhishthira listened in grief.

Young Abhimanyu breached the outer ramparts with incomparable valor, but the inner
sanctum of the cipher remains sealed behind cosmic gates.

THE RIDDLE OF THE FOUR FRAGMENTS
================================================================================

Four fragments of ancient military lore were recovered from Drona's command tent:

[FRAGMENT A: THE LAW OF THE SCRIBES]
"The scribes of the Kuru court keep one law: no counsel is written until the
speaker is named and the ancient word for spoke is set beside him."

[FRAGMENT B: THE FIRST VOICE]
"The first voice of the great song was the king who sat in darkness while the
field blazed — father of the hundred sons — who could inquire of the battle
but never see its light."

[FRAGMENT C: THE IDLE STROKES]
"Every scribe makes idle strokes before the first word, to steady the trembling
hand. No two hands strike the same number of idle marks."

[FRAGMENT D: THE REARRANGED TONGUE]
"Before any word ever reached the mechanical wheels, the narrator's own name,
joined to his formula, had already rearranged the tongue."
